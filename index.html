The problem we set out to solve

Residence maintenance is still run on chat messages and paper

When reports get lost, small faults turn into expensive breakdowns and nobody knows who was responsible. That's the gap our project addresses.

Lost reports

Faults sent over WhatsApp get buried and never reach the right person.

No accountability

No record of who was assigned, when they started, or whether the job was done.

Unidentified assets

"The geyser upstairs" isn't an asset ID. Repairs get delayed by guesswork.

Reactive, not preventive

Without service history, equipment only gets attention once it fails.

Features

What we built

NFC Communal Asset Identification

Identify shared residence assets by tapping their NFC tags. Access relevant asset information and simplify maintenance reporting for communal facilities such as elevators, washing machines, projectors and shared TVs.

Fault reporting

A guided form with category, priority from Low to Urgent, and a description. Residents can follow it until it's closed.

Work Order Assignment

Assign maintenance reports according to artisan specialisation and available capacity. Queue processing helps manage pending assignments when artisans reach their workload limits.

Asset Lifecycle & History

Maintain records of asset details, locations and previous maintenance activities. Review repair history to understand recurring problems and keep asset information organised

Scheduled Notices

Warn residents ahead of water shutdowns, load shedding, fumigation or pool closures, by building or area.

Compliance & Safety Monitoring

Monitor inspection requirements and certificate due dates. Identify compliant, due-soon and overdue assets so administrators can prioritise inspections and follow-up work.

Interactive Residence Flow Map

Visualise maintenance operations through an interactive flow map. Follow the movement of maintenance reports between workflow stages and gain a clearer view of ongoing maintenance activities.

Who it's for

One platform. Three connected roles.

MainTenant connects residents, artisans and administrators in one maintenance workflow, from reporting faults and assigning repairs to tracking progress and managing residence assets.

Report it in under a minute

No more chasing the house committee. Residents report faults from their phone and always know where things stand.

  • Submit maintenance reports for residence faults
  • Track active reports and their status
  • See upcoming shutdowns and maintenance notices

A clear job list, every day

Artisans see exactly what's assigned to them, where it is, and how urgent it is.

  • Personal task queue sorted by priority
  • One-tap status updates, including Awaiting Parts
  • Add work notes and observations to the asset record

Manage the residence from one place

Admins manage users, rooms and assets, and make sure nothing falls through the cracks.

  • Review and approve artisan sign-up requests
  • Assign reports and schedule planned maintenance
  • Allocate rooms and manage residence records
  • Manage assets and access their maintenance history
  • Monitor maintenance compliance statuses
